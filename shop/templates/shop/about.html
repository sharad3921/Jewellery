OUR JOURNEY

The Story of Tishyya

ESTABLISHED IN 2020

Redefining Fine Jewelry

Tishyya was born out of a desire to create jewelry that speaks to the soul. We believe that fine jewelry is more than just an accessory—it is a form of self-expression, a carrier of memories, and a reflection of personal style. Our collections are designed for the modern individual who values craftsmanship, detail, and timeless aesthetics.

Every piece in our catalog is meticulously curated, designed, and finished by skilled craftsmen. From the selection of premium-grade gold and sterling silver to the setting of precious gemstones, we maintain an uncompromising standard of quality.

OUR CORE PILLARS

Values We Shine By

01

Exquisite Craftsmanship

We blend centuries-old traditional jewelry-making techniques with modern, precise architectural designs to create silhouettes that stand out.

02

Ethically Sourced

We are committed to sustainability. 100% of our precious metals are recycled, and every diamond or gemstone is sourced from conflict-free mines.

03

Accessible Luxury

By operating purely online and designing in-house, we eliminate traditional retail markups, offering premium quality without the premium price tag.

MEET THE FOUNDERS

Driven by Passion

Tishyya was founded by a team of design enthusiasts and gemologists who shared a vision of creating a transparent, luxury-focused, and sustainable jewelry brand. Our studio operates from BKC, Mumbai, where we continuously design collections that blend Eastern elegance with Western minimalism.
